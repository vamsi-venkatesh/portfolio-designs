About

I build AI products, and I run them.

I design, build and run AI products end to end: the product, the agents, the servers and the tests that prove it works. Eight products so far, seven of them live. I set the standard each one is measured against, and nothing ships until I have checked it myself.

How I work

I own the whole stack.

Built, deployed and debugged in production. The live products run on servers I administer, behind deploy gates I wrote: full test suites, checksum sweeps, rendered-frame checks, rollback roots.

I hold the standard.

I built the evaluation instrument my agents are measured with, and every claim on my sites is gated on a threshold declared in advance and checked on the served artefact, never on the source file.

I ship with a team of agents, and I sign every release.

Multi-agent pipelines are how I ship faster than one pair of hands. I plan the work, review every output, and sign off before anything reaches a user. The send stays human.
