    How I work

    1. I own the whole stack.Built, deployed and debugged in production. The live products run on servers I administer, behind deploy gates I wrote: full test suites, checksum sweeps, rendered-frame checks, rollback roots.
    2. I hold the standard.I built the evaluation instrument my agents are measured with, and every claim on my sites is gated on a threshold declared in advance and checked on the served artefact, never on the source file. Verified numbers or a dash.
    3. I ship with a team of agents, and I sign every release.Multi-agent pipelines are how I ship faster than one pair of hands. I plan the work, review every output, and sign off before anything reaches a user. The send stays human.
