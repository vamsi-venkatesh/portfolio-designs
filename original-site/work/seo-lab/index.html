SEO Lab — search demand into useful public products.

A private research and product-development initiative I am building to identify genuine search demand, validate promising opportunities and turn the strongest ones into useful public tools. The internal system stays private; this page documents only public products, principles and verified outcomes.

The purpose

SEO Lab began with a straightforward question: can I build products around what people demonstrably need instead of guessing what might attract attention? It brings product research, organic discovery, validation and iteration into one private working environment. The goal is not to manufacture pages for algorithms. It is to find a real problem and build the most useful, honest answer I can—sometimes a tool, sometimes a product, sometimes a carefully researched resource.

The internal machinery is private. The public standard is simple: useful products and claims that can be verified.

First major live proof: UnboundPDF

UnboundPDF is the first major product shipped through this initiative: 32 PDF tools that run inside the browser. People can merge, split, convert, edit, optimise and secure documents without an account, a watermark or uploading the files to a server.

Privacy is part of the product architecture rather than a marketing add-on. The files stay on the user's device, and the interface explains when a document needs repair, unlocking or OCR instead of silently failing. The public product is live; discovery, documentation and authority-building are the current growth work.

Now live: Conxto Résumé Studio

Conxto Résumé Studio is now a live Conxto product. Conxto provides the career-product and résumé-engine foundation, while SEO Lab supports the research and organic-discovery direction around it.

The public experience is free, requires no login, adds no watermark and places no limit on résumés or downloads. It includes guided building and import, 37 templates, PDF and DOCX export, browser-local storage and an honest ATS check. The product is shipped; search discovery, content coverage and growth are the continuing work.

What remains private

SEO Lab has its own internal research environment. Its agents, tools, methods, operating workflows, private data and infrastructure are not public portfolio material. That boundary protects the work while keeping the case study honest: visitors can inspect the products and their results without receiving a blueprint of the system behind them.

How I measure progress

Shipping is the first test, not the last one. A product still has to be useful, discoverable and trustworthy. I separate verified demand from assumptions, label work in progress clearly, and publish only numbers that have a source. More search-led tools and products are planned, but they will appear here only after there is something real to inspect.

UnboundPDF and Conxto Résumé Studio are live. Everything else remains a plan until it ships.
