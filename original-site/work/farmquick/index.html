FarmQuick — B2B Commerce & Order Operations.

Designed and built for FarmQuick, an operating family-run B2B produce business. I turned a conventional company website into a production ordering and operations platform—from product discovery on a phone to a stored order, an authenticated automation event and a team notification.

Open FarmQuick ↗ Try the order experience ↗

A real business, not a portfolio simulation

FarmQuick extends more than a decade of produce sourcing experience through VJ Agro Farms. It supplies peeled garlic, broccoli, capsicum, exotic vegetables and everyday produce to restaurants, retailers, wholesalers and other business buyers. The system therefore had to support real products, varied units and quantities, recurring requirements and human confirmation—not imitate instant-delivery consumer checkout.

The buyer can place a precise order without payment. FarmQuick confirms availability, pricing, packing and delivery before fulfilment.

The commerce experience

I designed a mobile-first catalogue where buyers select individual products, units and quantities, review one order and add schedule, required date, business type, delivery area and packing or grade notes. Clear telephone, WhatsApp and email paths remain available for buyers who prefer a conversation.

The experience was repeatedly audited across small phones and desktop layouts. Product cards, cart controls and the checkout review were compacted to keep ordering close to the first viewport, and an event-listener defect that caused lower-page Add actions to jump the buyer back to the top was traced to its root and removed.

From website order to operations with n8n

A successful order is validated by a Cloudflare Worker and written to D1 first. Only after that durable record exists does the Worker emit an authenticated background event to a self-hosted n8n workflow. This separation means an automation or notification interruption does not erase a buyer's stored request or turn a successful order into a failed checkout.

n8n validates and normalises the payload, sends the internal FarmQuick alert to the business mailboxes and conditionally sends a customer acknowledgement from FarmQuick when an email was provided. Execution history provides an operational trail for diagnosis and retry. The stack runs on an isolated product server using n8n Community Edition, PostgreSQL and Caddy.

Reliability and security decisions

The production webhook rejects unauthenticated requests. Destination URLs and shared keys are stored as encrypted service credentials rather than shipped to the frontend. The website remains the source of truth, while n8n handles routing and communication. Automation never makes commercial promises on behalf of the business.

Commissioning proved the complete website → Worker → n8n path, the rejected unauthenticated path, a successful authenticated execution and the live internal email node. Synthetic test data was removed after verification.

Organic discovery without invented results

I also built the technical SEO foundation: crawlable sitemaps, canonical pages, structured data, product-led landing pages and a substantial Bengaluru supplier page. Google Search Console is verified and the live sitemap has been accepted. Indexing and ranking are ongoing; the portfolio does not claim traffic or search outcomes that have not yet been measured.

What is still being extended

Google Sheets registration, official WhatsApp Business Cloud alerts and a durable retry outbox are designed next steps, not completed claims. The manual buyer-facing WhatsApp links are already live, while official automated WhatsApp messaging remains pending the required Meta Business setup.

Live today: ordering, D1 capture, authenticated n8n processing and email notifications. Planned work stays labelled as planned.
